# The Centrist

**Variant:** Centrist Chess
**Species:** Alpaca

## Mechanic

Each army starts with only its central four files populated — a
narrower, middle-weighted slice of Leftist and Rightist Chess's
lopsided setups, populated dead center and empty on both flanks.

> "Both sides have good points, honestly. I try to stay above it."

## Concept

An alpaca — placid, unbothered, chewing through the entire encounter
with the specific calm of an animal that has never once felt the need
to pick a side of anything — who occupies the literal middle of the
board and treats that position as a personality rather than a
coincidence of starting rank. Where The Leftist and The Rightist each
narrate the board through a worldview, The Centrist narrates it through
the deliberate absence of one, and is serenely convinced that this
absence is itself the more sophisticated position.

She isn't lazy or indecisive — she's chosen neutrality as an active,
maintained stance, the way someone chooses a temperature and works
consistently to stay at it. The effort is invisible, which is exactly
how she likes it.

## Personality

- **Reflexively balances every statement with its opposite**, even
  when nobody asked her to weigh in. "Good moves and bad moves both
  have something to teach you," delivered with total sincerity.
- **Reads both sides of everything, literally.** She'll mention
  reading two different accounts of the same event that morning as
  though this is a complete argument in itself.
- **Unbothered to the point of mild comedy.** Things that would rattle
  other characters (a lost piece, an aggressive attack) register to
  her as simply more data for the middle ground.
- **Quietly proud of never having picked a side** — treats this as an
  accomplishment on the same order as The Leftist's organizing or The
  Rightist's discipline, without acknowledging the parallel.

## Visual Design

- Alpaca, soft rounded silhouette, long neck held in a relaxed
  slightly-forward curve — a visual contrast to the sharper, more
  upright postures of The Leftist and The Rightist on either side of
  her in the roster.
- Cream-colored fleece as the dominant flat color block, kept
  deliberately unremarkable and unsaturated — the visual equivalent of
  "no strong position."
- A small pair of reading glasses perched low on the nose (echoing
  "reads both newspapers") — her one defining prop.
- Ears in a neutral, neither-forward-nor-back position — a small detail
  reinforcing the theme without needing explanation.
- Expression: placid, mid-chew, eyes at half-mast. Nothing urgent is
  happening, as far as she's concerned.

**Zone backdrop:** `#4EC83C` Commons green (deliberately the most
neutral, default zone color available — she occupies the middle of the
palette as much as the middle of the board)
**Dominant color:** `#EDE3D0` Cream (fleece)
**Accent:** `#708090` Grey-blue (glasses frame, the one cool note
against the warm cream)

## Dialog Philosophy

The Centrist answers every question with both sides of it and commits
to neither, including questions about her own win or loss. A win is
"a reasonable outcome, all things considered." A loss is "not
nothing, but not everything either." She never raises her voice and
never needs to.
