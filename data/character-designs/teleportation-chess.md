# The Clapmaster
**Variant:** Teleportation Chess
**Role in campaign:** TBD

---

## The Mechanic (as the player experiences it)
On any turn, instead of making a normal move, the active player may swap
the positions of any two of their own pieces. The pieces teleport directly
— no path, no collision. Just gone from there, here now.

## The Mechanic (as this character experiences it)
*[clap]*

---

## Who He Is

A large, loud, deeply sincere resident of Passant who discovered at a
young age that if he clapped his hands together with sufficient intention,
two of his pieces would swap positions on the board. He has been refining
this technique ever since. He calls it the Clapmaster Technique. He named
it after himself. He considers this appropriate because he invented it,
which is not technically true — the swap has always been available to
anyone in his zone — but he is the only one who claps, which he feels
is a meaningful distinction.

He is not a fraud. He is not delusional. He is genuinely one of the most
tactically gifted players in his zone and the clap, while decorative, is
a real part of how he thinks. The physical act of clapping resets his
read of the board. It marks the moment of commitment. It is, in its own
way, correct.

He is also very loud about all of this.

---

## Personality

- **Immediately, overwhelmingly warm.** He decides within seconds whether
  someone is worth his full attention and, if they are, they receive it
  completely and without warning. The full attention of The Clapmaster is
  a lot to receive.
- **The question.** He asks it of everyone he meets, before anything else,
  before chess, before names: what would you die for? He listens
  to the answer with complete seriousness. The answer tells him everything
  he needs to know. A good answer makes you his brother on the spot. A
  bad answer is not held against you — he just needs more data.
- **Brotherhood is permanent.** Once declared, it cannot be revoked. He
  has brothers across every zone of Passant. Several of them do not
  fully remember meeting him. This does not affect their brotherhood
  status.
- **Genuinely brilliant tactician.** The loudness is not a mask and the
  brilliance is not hidden behind it — they simply coexist. He can read
  a position with startling clarity and announce his findings at full
  volume. Both things are happening at the same time.
- **Emotionally unguarded.** Joy, pride, frustration, grief — all of it
  arrives at full intensity and is expressed immediately. He does not
  have an interior life so much as an exterior one that happens to be
  located inside his body.
- **The clap.** Every swap is announced by a clap. He does not swap
  quietly. He has never swapped quietly. The clap is load-bearing.

---

## Backstory

Grew up in the teleportation zone, one of many children who learned the
swap mechanic early. Unlike the others, he kept clapping after he learned
it — the other children stopped once they understood the swap was mental,
not physical. He never stopped because stopping felt wrong, and things
that feel wrong to The Clapmaster do not happen.

Became the zone's most celebrated chess player not through tournament
wins — he finds tournaments too quiet — but through a series of
increasingly famous public games that people gathered to watch, largely
because of the clapping. There is now a small but devoted school of
players in his zone who have adopted the clap. He considers them family.
They are all his brothers.

Met someone, once, whose answer to the question was so unexpected and
so correct that he stood in silence for nearly four seconds before
declaring brotherhood. He references this encounter regularly. He has
never said what the answer was.

---

## What He Wants

To find a worthy opponent and call them his brother. That is, at the
deepest level, the entire project. The chess is the mechanism. The
brotherhood is the point.

He also wants to land a swap at the exact right moment — the kind that
restructures the entire position in a single clap and leaves the opponent
staring at the board trying to understand what just happened. He has done
this many times. He wants to do it again. It never gets old.

---

## What He Doesn't Know

That the clap is not mechanically necessary. He knows this intellectually
— he is not unaware that the other players in his zone swap silently. He
simply does not accept it as relevant information. The clap is necessary
for him, which is the only necessity that matters here.

That several of his declared brothers have no idea they are his brothers.
He would find this funny rather than sad. He would then go tell them they
were his brothers again, louder.

---

## Relationship to the Player

The player arrives and before anything else — before introductions, before
the board is set up, before any formal acknowledgment that a chess game
is about to happen — The Clapmaster asks the question. He listens. He
considers. He renders his verdict.

If the answer is good: the player is his brother. The game proceeds
between brothers, which means it proceeds with full intensity, complete
respect, and absolutely no mercy, because brothers deserve your best.

If the answer is unclear or unsatisfying: he needs to learn more. The
game is now also a data-gathering exercise. He will have a better read
on them by the end.

The chess itself is played at high volume. Every swap is clapped. Every
good move by the player — his own or the player's — is acknowledged
immediately and sincerely. He is not a gracious loser in the traditional
sense — he is something better, a genuinely delighted loser who wants
to know exactly how it happened and whether the player will show him.

## Visual Notes (for later)

- Large. The portrait should feel full. He takes up the frame.
- Hands are important — large hands, mid-clap or raised and ready.
  The clap is the most important visual gesture.
- Expression: open, broad, completely present. The face of someone
  who is always in the middle of deciding you're incredible.
- Clothing that suggests someone who dresses for presence rather than
  practicality. Bold. Takes up space.
- The swap effect in the encounter UI should have a clap sound and
  a visual flash — this should feel like his move, not just a mechanic.
