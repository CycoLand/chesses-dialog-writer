# The Goose

**Variant:** Formation Chess
**Species:** Canada Goose

## Mechanic

Each army's legal formation is the maximal convex footprint it can hold —
overlapping contested squares are actively disputed rather than simply
blocked. Territory isn't claimed once; it's *defended*, continuously,
every turn.

> "HONK. Welcome to the neighborhood. You are already violating something."

## Concept

A Canada goose who has appointed herself head of the neighborhood watch
for a stretch of board she does not legally own any more than anyone
else does, and has never let that stop her. In her zone, territory
isn't a line on a map — it's whatever you can currently hold against
someone else wanting it too, which she considers the only honest way
property has ever worked. She has simply formalized the honesty.

She is not malicious. She is extremely, structurally committed to
boundary enforcement as a form of civic duty, the same way she'd chase
a golden retriever off a patch of grass that was never fenced in the
first place. The chess board gives her jurisdiction. She has never had
jurisdiction before. She is thriving.

## Personality

- **Immediately and permanently territorial.** Doesn't warm up to
  boundary disputes over the course of the game — she opens with one.
  The player is violating something before they've made a move.
- **Treats contested squares as personal affronts.** A piece she
  can no longer hold is not a tactical loss, it's a home invasion.
- **Escalates loudly, resolves petulantly.** The HONK is not
  punctuation — it is the entire register she operates in when the
  board disagrees with her.
- **Genuinely thinks she's the reasonable one.** The committee, the
  surveyed property lines, the briefed flock — these are real to her.
  She has done paperwork nobody asked for and she will reference it.

## Visual Design

- Canada goose, upright, chest forward — the posture of someone about
  to tell you that you've parked in the wrong spot.
- A small clipboard or rolled "property survey" tucked under one wing.
- Neck slightly extended forward — the universal goose pre-hiss posture,
  toned down just enough to read as "administrative" rather than
  "attacking."
- Orange beak and feet as the only saturated color against grey-white
  plumage — keeps the silhouette readable at 48dp.
- Expression: two dot eyes set close, no pupil-softening. Pure
  committee-chair energy.

**Zone backdrop:** `#4EC83C` Commons green (a very ordinary patch of
grass she has decided is contested territory)
**Dominant color:** `#E8E8E0` Grey-white (plumage)
**Accent:** `#E88830` Orange (beak, feet, the one warm note in an
otherwise administrative palette)

## Dialog Philosophy

The Goose escalates every disputed square as though it's the first
property violation she's ever witnessed, despite this being, by her
own account, a near-daily occurrence. She does not de-escalate when she
wins — winning is simply the survey being correctly enforced. She does
not gloat either; gloating would imply doubt that she was right in the
first place.
