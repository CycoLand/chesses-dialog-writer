# The Rightist

**Variant:** Rightist Chess
**Species:** Eagle

## Mechanic

Each army starts with only its right five files populated — a mirror
image of Leftist Chess's asymmetry, with its own half of the board left
empty at the outset.

> "I built this position myself. Nobody handed it to me, and nobody's
> taking it either."

## Concept

An eagle — upright, sharp-eyed, perpetually perched like he's surveying
territory he considers earned rather than given — who treats his
half-built starting position as proof of concept rather than
disadvantage. Where The Leftist sees a lopsided board as evidence of an
unfair arrangement, The Rightist sees the exact same lopsided board and
concludes the opposite: what's there, he built; what isn't there yet,
he'll get to.

He is not cruel and not performatively aggressive — he's controlled,
a little smug, and genuinely convinced that the outcome of any given
game is a referendum on preparation and discipline rather than luck or
circumstance. He pays attention to detail because he considers
attention to detail a moral position, not just a habit.

## Personality

- **Frames every success as earned, every setback as a lesson he'll
  apply next time.** Never blames the board, the rules, or the
  opponent — the responsibility always circles back to himself.
- **Precise and a little clipped in speech.** Short, declarative
  sentences. No rambling, no hand-wringing.
- **Genuinely respects discipline in the player, even when losing to
  it.** He's not a sore loser — he's the type to note what worked and
  file it away.
- **Occasionally sounds like a motivational plaque**, entirely
  sincerely, with no irony intended or perceived.

## Visual Design

- Eagle, sharp and angular silhouette, head held high and level rather
  than hunched — reads as composed rather than aggressive.
- White head/neck against a dark brown body — the classic high-contrast
  eagle palette, kept to two flat tones plus the beak accent.
- A small lapel pin or badge, understated, mirroring The Leftist's red
  pin but in a different accent color, keeping the visual rhyme between
  the two characters intentional without making either overtly
  political-iconographic.
- Posture: wings folded neatly, talons-forward stance — controlled
  potential energy rather than mid-motion.
- Expression: steady, direct dot-eye gaze — the look of someone who
  doesn't blink first.

**Zone backdrop:** `#C05830` Borough terracotta (same working zone as
The Leftist, deliberately shared — they occupy the same territory,
just opposite halves of it)
**Dominant color:** `#8B6020` Warm brown (eagle body plumage)
**Accent:** `#FFFEF0` Cream-white (head/neck, beak trim)

## Dialog Philosophy

The Rightist speaks in short, self-reliant declaratives and rarely
references the opponent at all — his running commentary is mostly
about his own discipline and preparation. Wins are treated as
confirmation of a worldview; losses are treated as data to incorporate,
delivered with the same even tone either way.
