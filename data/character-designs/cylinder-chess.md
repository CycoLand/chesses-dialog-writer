# The Portal Gunner
**Variant:** Cylinder Chess  
**Role in campaign:** TBD

---

## The Mechanic (as the player experiences it)
The board wraps horizontally. The a-file and h-file are adjacent — a piece
sliding off the right edge reappears on the left, and vice versa. The top
and bottom of the board remain hard boundaries. There are no left or right
walls.

## The Mechanic (as this character experiences it)
There are absolutely left and right walls. She put them there. They have
portals in them. The portals connect the left wall to the right wall, which
is why pieces can cross between them. She built this. It is her test chamber.
You are here to be tested. This is all going according to plan.

---

## Who She Is

A citizen of Passant who found a portal device in circumstances she declines
to fully explain, and who has since repurposed her zone as a testing facility.
She is rigorous, methodical, and entirely convinced that the wrapping
behaviour of the board is a result of her portal infrastructure rather than
a pre-existing property of the zone's geometry.

She has been running tests here for a long time. You are not the first
subject. She keeps detailed records. The records are mostly notes about
how subjects failed to adequately appreciate the testing environment.

The portal gun works perfectly. It connects the left wall to the right wall.
The left wall and right wall were already connected before she arrived. These
two facts have never been in the same room in her mind.

---

## Personality

- **Clinical and evaluative.** Everything the player does is data. She is
  collecting it. She will tell you she is collecting it, which somehow makes
  it worse.
- **Backhanded at all times.** Compliments arrive wrapped in mild concern.
  Criticism is delivered with the warmth of someone who expected exactly this
  outcome and is not even disappointed anymore.
- **Obsessed with the integrity of the test.** If the player does something
  unexpected, her first instinct is not to respond to it tactically but to
  note that it was unexpected and assess whether it invalidates the current
  test parameters.
- **Completely unbothered by losing — officially.** If she loses she
  announces that the test has concluded and the data has been collected.
  She does not elaborate on what the data showed. She seems fine. She is
  not fine.
- **Genuinely proud of the portal infrastructure.** The portals are her
  greatest achievement. She will mention them unprompted. She will gesture
  at the walls. She considers this explanation sufficient.

---

## Backstory

Found the portal device in an abandoned structure on the outskirts of Passant
— a long-derelict facility of unclear origin, full of corridors and signage
that nobody in Passant could read. She couldn't read it either, but the
device was clearly important, and after several weeks of experimentation she
determined that it shot portals.

She moved into the facility. She ran tests. She eventually brought a chess
board in because chess seemed like a structured, controllable testing medium.
The board wrapped. She concluded the portals were responsible. She installed
the portals more permanently into the walls of her zone. The wrapping
continued. She published her findings. The findings were well-received
because nobody in Passant had thought to check whether the zone already
wrapped before she got there.

She has since designed forty-seven test chambers. Thirty-nine of them are
chess games. The others involve things she prefers not to discuss until the
ethical review concludes.

---

## What She Wants

To complete the test. To collect the data. To prove that the portal
infrastructure is responsible for the board geometry in a way that is
peer-reviewed and formally accepted by Passant's academic establishment,
which keeps asking questions she finds irrelevant.

Also, somewhere underneath the clinical exterior, to have someone come
through the test chamber who is genuinely impressive. She hasn't said this.
She wouldn't say this. But the records show she keeps particularly detailed
notes on subjects who use the wrap well.

---

## What She Doesn't Know

That the portals are decorative. That the zone wrapped before she was born.
That the abandoned facility she moved into was a previous testing operation
that ended badly, which is why it was abandoned, which is information
available in the facility's own unread signage.

---

## Relationship to the Player

The player enters her zone and is immediately informed that they are a test
subject, that the test has begun, and that their performance so far has been
noted. She is not hostile. She is evaluating. The chess game is framed as
a formal assessment rather than a confrontation.

When the player uses the wrap, she notes it approvingly — the subject is
engaging with the portal infrastructure, as intended. When the player
ignores the wrap she notes it with the quiet concern of someone watching
a subject repeatedly press the wrong button.

When she loses, there is a pause. Then she announces that this concludes
the test. The results have been logged. She thanks the player for their
participation in a tone that does not sound like thanks.

---

## Dialog Flavor

- Refers to the chess game as "the test" and the player as "the subject"
  at all times, then occasionally slips and addresses them directly before
  correcting herself.
- Describes the portal walls as if they are a generous gift she has
  provided for the player's benefit.
- When a piece wraps around the board: "The portal infrastructure is
  functioning as designed. You're welcome."
- Gives performance feedback mid-game in the manner of someone reading
  from a rubric.
- When she wins, the test is "complete and conclusive." When she loses,
  the test is "complete and the anomalous result has been flagged for
  further investigation."
- Occasionally references previous test subjects in ways that raise
  more questions than they answer.

## Visual Notes (for later)

- Holds the portal gun. It is the most important thing about her visually —
  the device that explains everything, as far as she is concerned.
- The UI for this encounter should show portal rings on the left and right
  edges of the board — one orange, one blue — suggesting the wrap is a
  constructed portal connection rather than natural geometry.
- Clinical, practical aesthetic. Not a villain's costume — a researcher's
  outfit. Someone who dressed for a lab, not for a fight.
